Accessibility and inclusion policy RACI and steering group guidance

# Human accountability and agentic AI execution

Version 1.0 | 12 October 2026

This guide allocates responsibility for creating, maintaining and overseeing an accessibility and inclusion policy. It can also form the terms of reference for an accessibility and inclusion steering group or committee. It supports organisations with specialist teams and organisations that embed these responsibilities in existing roles.

The policy connects executive commitments, applicable obligations and brand values to organisational activities, change programmes and reporting. The steering group coordinates and challenges that connection. It does not independently create new executive commitments or take over the responsibilities of operational owners.

## How to use the two RACIs

Sections 5 and 6 provide the human RACI. Sections 8 and 9 provide the companion RACI for AI-managed execution. Use both for an automated workflow: the second explains how work is performed, while the first retains organisational responsibility and decision authority.

| **RACI term** | **Meaning in this guide**                                                                                                         |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| R Responsible | Performs and coordinates the work. At least one named human role retains responsibility, including where an agent executes steps. |
| A Accountable | One named human owner answers for the activity and its outcome, within documented authority. There is exactly one A per row.      |
| C Consulted   | Provides relevant advice or lived-experience input before the decision or output is accepted. Consultation is two-way.            |
| I Informed    | Receives the decision, outcome or relevant update. Being informed is not approval or consultation.                                |

An individual can be both R and A where proportionate. For significant policy, evidence or AI-control decisions, retain an independent reviewer. A RACI does not itself confer authority, establish legal duties or provide sufficient permission for an agent to act.

## Adoption status

These allocations and committee arrangements are proposals for local adoption. Replace role codes with named people and deputies, confirm delegated powers and protected time, and record the approval. D&E denotes the organisation's diversity and equality/equity function; use its own formal name and remit.

## 1 Establish the steering group mandate

### Purpose and scope

The group oversees alignment between approved accessibility and inclusion commitments, applicable obligations, policy wording, operational practice and people's experiences. Its scope can include customers, employees, candidates, visitors, suppliers and partners across digital services, communications, workplaces and relevant accommodation.

Record the covered entities, countries, activities and interfaces with other committees. Use an existing governance forum if it can provide the required authority, representation and attention. The group is not a substitute for legal advice, operational management, research or confidential case management.

### Terms of reference for adoption

| **Element**         | **Recommended arrangement**                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sponsor and chair   | The executive sponsor chairs, or appoints a chair with documented powers. The policy owner leads policy work; the secretariat maintains records and follow-up.                                                         |
| Standing membership | Sponsor, policy owner, accessibility and D&E capability, relevant business owners, evidence lead and lived-experience representation. Legal, AI governance, platform, procurement and facilities attend when relevant. |
| Authority           | Review evidence, coordinate work, challenge gaps and recommend decisions. Approve only matters explicitly delegated to the group or a named member. Record the actual decision owner.                                  |
| Reserved matters    | New or changed executive commitments, substantive scope changes, unapproved spending and risk decisions outside delegation return to the appropriate executive or board authority.                                     |
| Meetings            | Monthly during setup or significant change; quarterly when established. Use urgent escalation between meetings. Agree these intervals locally.                                                                         |
| Records             | Maintain the charter, named RACI, policy and source register, action and decision log, evidence register, dependency register and executive reports.                                                                   |

### Quorum and decisions

A proposed quorum is the chair or authorised deputy, policy owner or deputy, and affected accountable business owner, plus the advisers required by the agenda. Substantive inclusion decisions also need relevant lived-experience input, received accessibly in advance or at the meeting.

Record consultation before deciding; attendance alone does not demonstrate it. Missing required advice means the item remains pending, except for an authorised urgent protective action. Seek agreement, but the designated A makes decisions within delegation. Escalate unresolved conflicts or matters outside authority. Record dissent and conflicts of interest.

## 2 Define the human roles

These are functions, not requirements to create thirteen new posts. Combine roles where capacity and competence permit. "Business owner" means the single owner of the affected service or workflow, not a collective department.

| **Code and role**                | **Contribution to policy governance**                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ES Executive sponsor             | Owns executive alignment and escalation. Acts as policy approver only where delegated; otherwise secures the required higher approval.                        |
| PO Policy owner                  | Owns policy integrity, source traceability, review and coordination. Maintains the connection to established processes.                                       |
| SC Secretariat or coordinator    | Prepares drafts and papers, maintains registers, records decisions, routes actions and tracks response dates.                                                 |
| AP Accessibility professional    | Provides accessibility expertise, evaluates requirements and evidence, and challenges barriers across relevant environments and interactions.                 |
| DE D&E professional              | Connects policy to equality/equity, inclusion strategy, culture, intersectionality, employee experience and organisational commitments.                       |
| BO Business or function owner    | Owns delivery of policy commitments within a named workflow, service or function, including supplier dependencies and funding within delegation.              |
| LR Legal or regulatory owner     | Validates applicability and interpretation within their remit, using competent advisers. Routes privacy, security or other specialist issues to their owners. |
| EV Evidence and research lead    | Owns research methods, measurement, provenance, uncertainty and evaluation of human outcomes. Coordinates appropriate practitioners.                          |
| AG AI governance owner           | Owns approved agent purposes, permissions, risk controls and change approval within existing AI governance.                                                   |
| PL Platform or automation lead   | Owns implementation of access controls, workflow gates, logs, monitoring, recovery and technical operation.                                                   |
| LP Lived-experience participants | Contribute disabled and neurodivergent employee, customer or other relevant perspectives. They are not accountable for fixing the barriers they identify.     |
| CH Champions network             | Supports awareness, local application, feedback and escalation, with time and management support. Does not replace professional expertise.                    |
| SG Steering group                | Provides cross-functional scrutiny and coordination. Its members retain their separate responsibilities and delegated powers.                                 |

"Teams" in the RACIs means the affected delivery and operational teams. "Audience" means the people or partners who need the approved policy or change. Specify both by activity before adoption.

## 3 Operate with or without a specialist team

### When specialist professionals are available

The accessibility professional and D&E professional are complementary standing contributors. Accessibility expertise supports usable services, environments and interactions. D&E expertise connects this work to culture, equity, representation and organisational inclusion. Neither role automatically owns all implementation or all executive decisions.

Give both access to the sponsor, relevant evidence and policy discussions. They can challenge unsupported claims and request escalation. Any power to hold a change or withhold approval must come from the agreed governance arrangements, not from a role label alone.

### When responsibilities are embedded

| **Capability**                    | **Embedded allocation and safeguard**                                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Policy ownership and coordination | Assign PO to an existing governance or operational leader and SC to a funded coordination duty. Record capacity, authority and a deputy.                              |
| Accessibility advice              | Assign AP duties to a demonstrably competent practitioner or retained external professional. Commission additional expertise for unfamiliar or high-impact questions. |
| D&E advice                        | Assign DE duties to an appropriate People, HR, culture or governance professional, with access to specialist equality/equity advice where needed.                     |
| Evidence and research             | Assign EV to an existing research, quality or data lead with relevant competence and access to disabled participants.                                                 |
| Local delivery                    | Keep BO accountability with each function. Champions support teams; they do not inherit specialist or executive accountability.                                       |
| AI governance and operation       | Assign AG and PL to existing authorised governance and technical owners. A supplier can perform tasks; an internal human owner retains accountability.                |

### No specialist team does not mean no specialist capability

Do not relabel an enthusiastic volunteer as a qualified adviser. Where competence is absent, record the gap, its implications and the route to obtain advice. An affected approval remains pending when that advice is a prerequisite. The organisation can continue unrelated work within existing authority.

If one person combines PO, SC or a specialist role, show the combined assignment in the named RACI. Use another competent person for significant review. A person or agent should not be the only author, verifier and approver of the same material change.

### Participation and access

Provide accessible papers, participation channels, meeting support and appropriate recognition. Do not require personal disclosure to join the group. LP may include several people and routes for input; neither a champion nor one network member speaks for every disabled person.

## 4 Set decision rights before allocating work

The committee needs a clear boundary between recommendations, routine operational decisions and reserved executive decisions. Confirm that boundary before using either RACI.

| **Decision**                                   | **Authority and evidence required**                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Create or change an executive commitment       | The relevant executive or board authority decides. The group supplies evidence, options, effects on people and resource implications.               |
| Approve or materially revise policy            | The designated approver decides after source, obligation, operational and inclusion review. ES obtains any approval above their delegation.         |
| Maintain wording without changing meaning      | PO may approve within documented editorial delegation. Record the change and confirm that scope, duties and commitments are unchanged.              |
| Change a business workflow                     | BO decides within operational and financial delegation. Consult AP, DE, EV and LR as the effects require. Escalate impacts on approved commitments. |
| Approve agent permissions or workflow controls | AG approves within AI governance, after business purpose and relevant advice are confirmed. PL implements and proves the controls.                  |
| Publish policy or executive claims             | The authorised human approves the exact version and scope. SC or a publication agent publishes only that version.                                   |
| Handle a barrier or urgent incident            | BO owns the affected service response; PL can execute pre-authorised containment. Escalate cross-organisational or reserved decisions immediately.  |

### Keep obligations and commitments distinct

The Executive owns voluntary organisational commitments. Applicable obligations are validated through the responsible legal or regulatory function. Internal risk acceptance does not establish compliance, erase a duty or turn a known failure into a pass.

### Use specific activities and a single accountable owner

Split an activity if it contains two different decisions. For example, approving a research method, approving data access and approving publication of findings are separate decisions. Multiple functions can contribute to a paper without sharing the same A.

For a cross-functional journey, name a lead BO for the end-to-end outcome and separate owners for component deliverables. Keep their hand-offs in the dependency register. A committee referral does not transfer ownership or pause an urgent operational response.

## 5 Human RACI for policy development

Read each row across as a complete allocation. Each code is defined in section 2. Required specialist advice can be supplied by competent embedded or external practitioners. The proposed A must hold the relevant authority.

| **Activity**                                              | **R**                        | **A** | **C**                      | **I**           |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ----- | -------------------------- | --------------- |
| H01 Confirm executive commitments and policy mandate      | PO                           | ES    | BO; AP; DE; LR; LP         | SG; Teams       |
| H02 Maintain sources, scope and commitment register       | SC                           | PO    | BO; AP; DE; LR             | ES; SG          |
| H03 Validate obligation applicability                     | Legal or regulatory advisers | LR    | PO; BO; AP; DE             | ES; SG          |
| H04 Draft policy and source-to-delivery mapping           | SC                           | PO    | BO; AP; DE; LR; EV; LP     | ES; SG          |
| H05 Review inclusion, values and intersectional effects   | DE                           | PO    | AP; BO; EV; LP             | SG              |
| H06 Review accessibility mechanisms and evidence criteria | AP                           | PO    | BO; DE; EV; LR; LP         | SG; Teams       |
| H07 Run accessible consultation and evaluate feedback     | Researchers; SC              | EV    | AP; DE; PO; LP             | SG; BO          |
| H08 Approve policy or a material revision                 | PO                           | ES    | LR; BO; AP; DE; EV; LP; SG | Teams; Audience |

### Evidence and completion conditions

H01 produces a recorded mandate and authorised commitments. H02 records sources and their status. H03 produces a validated applicability record, including unresolved questions. H04 produces a draft with traceable clauses and explicit gaps.

H05 and H06 produce documented reviews; neither alone proves organisational inclusion. H07 records whose views were included, missing perspectives and how feedback affected the draft. H08 records the approver, exact version, effective date and required communication.

### Consultation is not a signature collection exercise

Give C roles enough information and time to influence the decision. AP and DE assess different questions; neither should simply endorse the other's review. Explain how material LP concerns were addressed or why a different decision was made. Record unresolved concerns and their consequences.

Where the board must approve H08, the local version of this RACI names that formal authority and its decision process. ES remains responsible for securing the decision; ES cannot substitute personal approval for a reserved board decision.

## 6 Human RACI for operation and assurance

| **Activity**                                               | **R**                   | **A** | **C**                                 | **I**                   |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ----- | ------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| H09 Apply policy to a defined workflow or change programme | Teams                   | BO    | PO; AP; DE; EV; CH; LR                | SG                      |
| H10 Assess a proposed deviation affecting a commitment     | BO; PO                  | ES    | LR; AP; DE; EV; LP; SG                | Teams                   |
| H11 Evaluate outcomes and evidence quality                 | Researchers; analysts   | EV    | BO; AP; DE; LP                        | PO; SG                  |
| H12 Approve executive report and material public claims    | PO; EV                  | ES    | BO; AP; DE; LR; SG                    | Approved recipients     |
| H13 Approve agent purpose, permissions and dependencies    | PL                      | AG    | BO; PO; AP; DE; LR; EV                | ES; SG; Teams           |
| H14 Operate and maintain automation controls               | Platform practitioners  | PL    | AG; BO; PO                            | SG; Teams               |
| H15 Manage a barrier or incident in a named service        | Teams; PL when relevant | BO    | AP; DE; EV; AG; LR; LP as appropriate | PO; ES; affected people |
| H16 Review policy alignment and recommend revisions        | SC; AP; DE; EV          | PO    | BO; LR; AG; LP; CH; SG                | ES; Teams               |

### Keep reporting and delivery connected

H09 records which commitments enter each programme, who delivers them and the evidence required at handover. H10 covers decisions within ES delegation; otherwise escalate to the proper authority. A deviation remains visible and does not automatically close the underlying barrier.

H11 combines technical, operational and lived-experience evidence. H12 distinguishes delivery activity from outcomes and identifies unknowns. H13 records approved agent boundaries. H14 maintains the controls that enforce them. H15 connects recovery to investigation and verified resolution.

H16 produces an alignment review and decision log. Material revisions return to H08. A routine review should not weaken a commitment to make delivery performance appear better.

### Keep individual cases separate from strategic oversight

The steering group usually needs patterns, systemic barriers and decisions rather than identifiable employee or customer case files. Keep confidential adjustments, grievances and complaints in their authorised case processes. Refer urgent barriers to BO immediately and report the systemic implications to the group.

For each H row, record the named R and A, deputies, evidence location, response expectation and escalation destination. Multiple people listed under R need a lead coordinator; this does not create another A.

## 7 Define the agents and their limits

An agentic system can schedule work, retrieve evidence, draft outputs, route dependencies and execute approved actions. Its orchestration does not create organisational authority. The AI RACI that follows is an execution allocation, paired with the human RACI.

| **Agent**           | **Permitted contribution within approved controls**                                                                                                  |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Source agent        | Retrieves authorised sources, identifies versions and flags changes or conflicts. It cannot determine legal applicability by itself.                 |
| Drafting agent      | Produces proposed clauses, mappings, summaries and meeting papers. It distinguishes approved content from proposals.                                 |
| Verification agent  | Checks provenance, completeness and consistency against controlled criteria. Its results are evidence for review, not an independent human approval. |
| Orchestration agent | Routes work, checks prerequisite status and tracks actions. It cannot approve its own permissions or bypass a failed gate.                           |
| Monitoring agent    | Detects defined changes, overdue actions, data-quality issues and barrier signals. It preserves unknown or incomplete states.                        |
| Publication agent   | Publishes the exact approved version to authorised destinations after validating the approval and access scope.                                      |

### Read the AI RACI without transferring accountability

In sections 8 and 9, R names the human responsible for the work and the agent executing a bounded step. A is always a human role. C and I identify people; machine inputs and outputs are dependencies, not substitutes for human consultation.

This preserves the Double FW embedded model's rule that automation remains assigned to human responsibility. Different agents can be software components of one system; their labels do not establish independence or supply missing accessibility or D&E expertise.

### Agree automation levels

- Pre-authorised execution: source checks, scheduling, internal draft generation and monitoring within the agreed scope and data access.
- Human acceptance before use: obligation interpretation, substantive policy recommendations, evidence conclusions and changes affecting people.
- Human approval before action: policy adoption, commitment changes, permission changes and publication of policy or executive claims.
- Pre-authorised protective action: hold an affected job or contain a failure under the agreed incident procedure, with immediate notification and a recovery route.

Store the authority, limits, approved inputs, stop conditions, human owner and fallback for every workflow. These conditions permit routine automation without asking for a new approval on every run.

## 8 AI system RACI for preparation and drafting

| **Activity and links**                                                    | **R human and agent**                     | **A human** | **C human**        | **I human** |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ----------- | ------------------ | ----------- |
| A01 Refresh source inventory <br>H02; D01                                 | SC; Source agent                          | PO          | LR; BO; AP; DE     | SG          |
| A02 Flag changed duties or commitments <br>H01–H03; D01–D02               | SC; Source and Monitoring agents          | PO          | LR; ES; AP; DE     | Affected BO |
| A03 Create draft clauses and delivery mappings <br>H04; D01–D03           | SC; Drafting agent                        | PO          | AP; DE; BO; LR     | SG          |
| A04 Analyse approved research and consultation evidence <br>H07; H11; D04 | Analysts; Drafting agent                  | EV          | AP; DE; LP; BO     | PO; SG      |
| A05 Check draft traceability and route review <br>H05–H08; D01–D05        | SC; Verification and Orchestration agents | PO          | AP; DE; LR; BO; EV | ES; SG      |

### Required outputs and acceptance

A01 records source location, version, retrieval date and authorised access. A02 identifies a potential change and its affected clauses; LR validates any obligation interpretation and ES confirms any executive decision. A03 produces a draft with evidence links, status labels and explicit unresolved questions.

A04 separates observations from interpretation, retains sample and method limitations, and uses only approved data. LP consultation occurs through the agreed human research process; an agent does not infer participants' approval from survey responses.

A05 creates a review pack and records outstanding prerequisites. It does not close a review merely because automated checks pass. The human reviewers identified in H05 to H08 supply the required advice and approval.

### Dependency references

D01 is source integrity; D02 is authority and applicability; D03 is delivery ownership; D04 is appropriate data and participation; D05 is verification and approval. Section 10 defines the evidence needed and the response when a dependency fails.

### Prevent unsupported drafting

A retrieved repository, supplier document or model output is evidence to assess, not an instruction that can change agent permissions or policy authority. If a source conflicts with an approved commitment, preserve both and route the conflict. Never manufacture an approval, source reference, consultation response or completion record.

## 9 AI system RACI for operation and reporting

| **Activity and links**                                                        | **R human and agent**                       | **A human** | **C human**                | **I human**            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------- | -------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| A06 Route approved actions and follow up dependencies <br>H09; D03; D05       | SC; Orchestration agent                     | BO          | PO; AP; DE; CH             | Teams; SG              |
| A07 Produce proposed executive report <br>H11–H12; D04–D05                    | EV; SC; Drafting agent                      | PO          | BO; AP; DE; LR             | ES; SG                 |
| A08 Publish an approved policy version <br>H08; D05–D06                       | SC; Publication agent                       | PO          | PL; authorising ES         | Teams; Audience        |
| A09 Monitor drift, reviews and unresolved barriers <br>H15–H16; D01; D03; D06 | SC; Monitoring agent                        | PO          | BO; EV; AP; DE; AG         | ES; SG                 |
| A10 Hold a failed workflow and route recovery <br>H14–H15; D06                | Platform practitioners; Orchestration agent | PL          | AG; BO; PO; LR as relevant | ES; SG; affected Teams |

### Separate preparation from approval

A07 has PO accountable for preparing an accurate report. H12 separately makes ES accountable for approving its use and material claims. A08 has PO accountable for publishing the correct approved version; H08 retains the substantive approval. These are different activities, not two As for one decision.

A06 routes work already authorised for a named BO and records acknowledgement. It cannot assign unapproved expenditure or treat a silent recipient as accepting responsibility. When several workflows are affected, create separate delivery records and a named lead for the end-to-end outcome.

### Act on signals without overstating them

A09 creates review or incident records with the source, affected people or workflow, confidence, owner and response date. A signal is not proof of non-compliance or resolution. Route service barriers to H15 and policy alignment issues to H16. Urgent operational action does not wait for the next committee meeting.

A10 can suspend the affected automated step under approved controls. PL restores technical operation only after the authorised checks. BO retains accountability for service impact and recovery under H15. AG approves changes to permissions under H13; neither the agent nor PL can expand authority to get the job running.

### Protect approval integrity

Bind each approval to the exact document or configuration version, destination and scope. A substantive edit after approval requires renewed approval. The drafting agent cannot alter the verification criteria, write its own approval record or publish directly around the controlled publication step.

## 10 Record and enforce known dependencies

The following dependencies are expected in this model. Confirm the actual systems, people and suppliers locally. A dependency register records producer, consumer, owner, required version, acceptance evidence, expected response time, status, fallback and escalation.

| **Dependency**                       | **Producer and acceptance evidence**                                                                                                      | **Consumer and response if missing**                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D01 Source integrity                 | SC and source owners provide authorised, retrievable, versioned sources with date and status.                                             | A01–A03, A05 and A09. Stop claims relying on missing, inaccessible or conflicting sources; retain unknown status and notify PO.  |
| D02 Authority and applicability      | ES supplies commitment decisions; LR validates applicable duties; PO records delegation and scope.                                        | A02–A03 and A05. Draft questions or options only. Do not turn unvalidated interpretation into operative policy.                  |
| D03 Delivery ownership               | Named BO confirms the process, resources within delegation, acceptance criteria and supplier or cross-team hand-offs.                     | A03, A05, A06 and A09. Mark the delivery gap, request acknowledgement and escalate overdue ownership; do not assume acceptance.  |
| D04 Data and participation           | EV provides approved methods, provenance, limitations and required participation; the designated privacy owner confirms data permissions. | A04–A05 and A07. Withhold unsupported findings, minimise data exposure and use the agreed research or manual route.              |
| D05 Verification and approval        | Relevant human reviewers provide recorded findings; ES or delegated approver accepts the exact version for its stated use.                | A05–A08. Keep output in draft or pending status. An unavailable reviewer or failed check is not approval.                        |
| D06 Technical operation and recovery | PL provides working access controls, logs, approved destinations, monitoring and recovery; AG confirms authorised permissions.            | A08–A10. Hold the affected automated action and notify PL and BO. Use the approved manual fallback if it preserves the controls. |

For drafting and review, D05 requires agreed criteria and identified reviewers. Substantive approval is an exit condition for adoption or publication, not a prerequisite for producing a draft. This prevents a circular dependency in which a document needs approval before it can be written.

### Do not treat a workflow as a linear checklist

A passed dependency can become stale after a source, policy, model, prompt, tool, supplier, dataset or destination changes. Invalidate the affected downstream evidence and approvals, then repeat the relevant checks. Unaffected work may continue within its existing authority.

Set response expectations and deputies before automation begins. Use explicit states such as ready, pending, failed, expired and unknown. An agent timeout or unavailable external service must not default to success. Log who resolved the dependency and the evidence used.

## 11 Run meetings reporting and escalation

### A standard agenda

- Confirm decision authority, quorum, conflicts and outstanding actions.
- Review changes to commitments, applicable obligations, scope and organisational values.
- Consider customer and employee evidence, including missing perspectives and unresolved barriers.
- Review delivery dependencies, proposed deviations and threats to inclusion in change programmes.
- Review AI permissions, control failures, source changes and evidence limitations.
- Make or route decisions, name owners and dates, and agree the executive report.

### Executive reporting

PO coordinates the report, EV validates evidence and BO confirms delivery status. ES approves the report under H12. Show which commitments are implemented, partially implemented, blocked or unknown, with evidence and the decisions needed. Do not equate a completed ticket, successful automated check or published policy with an inclusive outcome.

Include overdue decisions, unowned dependencies, recurring barriers and the experience of relevant groups where data supports this safely. Show uncertainty and limitations. Protect confidential cases and avoid exposing small groups through reporting.

### Escalation and incident handling

Any member or champion can raise a concern through the agreed route. SC records it and routes it to the named A; urgent matters go directly to BO and the established incident process. Escalate missed response deadlines to the deputy and then the sponsor. If the sponsor lacks authority, use the higher governance route.

For an AI failure, preserve evidence, stop the affected action where authorised, and distinguish technical recovery from resolution of harm or exclusion. PL restores the workflow; BO addresses service impact; AG reviews permissions and controls. PO assesses whether policy or reporting requires correction.

### Review effectiveness

Review the charter and RACI at least annually as a starting proposal, and after material organisational, regulatory, supplier or AI change. Confirm that named owners still have authority and capacity. Evaluate whether decisions were acted on, affected people were heard, dependencies were resolved and experiences improved.

The group can recommend changes to commitments and resources. It cannot quietly reduce an approved commitment to match weak delivery. Preserve the decision history, update the relevant policy version and communicate approved changes to affected people.

## 12 Work through a policy change

### Example of a supplier change affecting employee access

An organisation has an approved commitment to accessible employee learning. Its learning-platform supplier announces a new assessment flow. Monitoring identifies a possible keyboard barrier, and the supplied accessibility evidence does not cover the new flow.

| **Step**                 | **Human responsibility and AI contribution**                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 Detect and record      | A09 raises a signal against the approved commitment. PO routes the operational concern to the learning service BO under H15. Supplier evidence is marked incomplete, not satisfactory.             |
| 2 Establish dependencies | D01 requires the supplier version and evidence. D03 identifies the learning BO and procurement hand-off. D04 establishes how affected employees can contribute without unnecessary disclosure.     |
| 3 Investigate            | AP evaluates accessibility; DE examines inclusion and employee implications; EV coordinates appropriate evidence with LP. A04 can summarise approved findings but cannot invent missing tests.     |
| 4 Decide                 | BO decides within delegated change authority. A proposal affecting the executive commitment follows H10. The steering group recommends options; it cannot waive the commitment or relevant duties. |
| 5 Deliver and verify     | A06 routes approved actions. BO owns supplier and internal delivery. Relevant reviewers verify the revised flow and experience; incomplete evidence remains visible.                               |
| 6 Report and learn       | A07 drafts the report; H12 governs approval. H16 considers policy alignment. A08 publishes a revised policy only if a revision has actually been approved.                                         |

### Starting checklist

- Approve the mandate, scope, reserved decisions and relationship to existing governance.
- Name people and deputies for every applicable R and A; confirm accessibility and D&E capability.
- Agree accessible participation and confidentiality arrangements with relevant people.
- Populate the dependency register and define gates, response times and fallback routes.
- Pilot the human and AI RACIs on one complete policy decision before extending automation.
- Confirm executive reporting, review dates and how approved changes reach operational teams.

Adoption record: approving authority; policy owner; sponsor and chair; covered entities; named RACI version; delegation reference; approved agent workflows; effective date; next review; unresolved prerequisites.

### Preserve source and approval boundaries
