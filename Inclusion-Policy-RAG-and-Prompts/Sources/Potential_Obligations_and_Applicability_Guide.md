# Identifying potential obligations before drafting policy

Version 1.0 · 17 November 2026 · MIT · A preparation method for policy professionals

Use this guide to identify questions, sources and decisions needed for an organisational policy. It does not supply a universal list of applicable laws or approve legal conclusions. Practical support and independent drafting can continue while an affected legal question is unresolved.

## 1 Describe the organisation and decision

Record the actual legal entities, countries of operation and service, employment relationships, activities, products/services, customer groups, premises, suppliers, contracts and relevant dates. Identify the policy purpose and actual decision owner. Where AI is involved, describe the use, operator role and affected people; use of an AI drafting assistant alone does not classify every organisational service.

Keep unknown facts visible. Do not infer a jurisdiction from a language or apply a rule solely because the organisation has a website. An organisation can have different obligations for different activities and territories.

## 2 Select relevant questions and public guidance

Use [R01 in the public register](Public_Source_Register.md) to discover candidate frameworks, then retrieve only relevant specialist sources.

| Activity or question | Candidate route | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Products and consumer services | R02: EAA scope guide | Establish actual category, role, territory, dates, exceptions and national implementation. Do not assume every commercial website is covered. |
| Public-sector websites/apps | R03: WAD guidance | Verify the body, assets, national rules, cited baseline, statements and feedback requirements. |
| AI in organisational activities | R04: AI Act scope | Examine each actual use and role; employment, models, products and interfaces may raise different questions. |
| Audiovisual/media activities | R05: AVMSD routes | Classification, jurisdiction and national implementation require specialist review; making a video alone is not a classification. |
| Employment, recruitment and adjustments | R06: employment starting questions | Use actual relationship, country and facts. Do not transfer Great Britain rules automatically to Northern Ireland or another country. |
| Personal/disability-related evidence | R07: data ethics, with privacy/legal advice | Establish purpose, lawful processing arrangements, special-category questions, recipients and safeguards; ethical guidance does not establish legal permission. |
| Procurement and supplier evidence | R01, R02/R03 when relevant, and R09 | Inspect actual legal/contractual scope and product/version evidence. A supplier declaration or ACR is not a certificate of complete legal compliance. |
| Premises, events or accommodation | Local authoritative building, safety, equality and sector sources; R06/R18 only for relevant questions | This repository is not a building-code or safety catalogue. Missing coverage requires competent local advice, not a digital-only assumption. |

The R references are navigation aids. They are not enacted law and their original review dates must not be silently refreshed. Other equality, privacy, sectoral, contractual and local duties may require sources beyond this selected map.

## 3 Check primary sources and scope

For each candidate, identify the current authentic instrument or contractual provision, exact clause, jurisdiction, entity/operator and activity, relevant dates and transitional conditions, exceptions, applicable standard/version and legislation-specific citation where relevant. Record the check date and any inaccessible source. A proposal, published standard, cited standard and adopted organisational baseline are different states.

Use official publishers and regulator/national implementation material appropriate to the question. Where source currency or interpretation is unresolved, draft the question and affected options for a competent reviewer. Do not turn a repository summary, similar criterion title or prior organisation's assessment into a new legal determination.

## 4 Maintain an applicability register

This is part of the supporting clause/source register, not an additional mandatory policy output.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Candidate and source | Framework/provision, primary URL, exact locator, edition and check date |
| Organisation and activity | Entity, operator/relationship, territory, relevant product/service/people and dates |
| Applicability state | Confirmed applicable; excluded with a reason; or unresolved |
| Basis and limits | Relevant facts, conditions, exception/transition evidence, conflicting sources and coverage limits |
| Decision | Actual competent reviewer, authority, decision/evidence reference and date; otherwise pending |
| Policy consequence | Affected draft clause/process, next evidence/action and accountable function if known |
| Review trigger | Source, contract, activity, geography, supplier or relevant organisational change |

Begin candidates as unresolved. Only record confirmed applicability or an exclusion when supported by the required facts, sources and genuine review. Missing evidence is not an exemption. Do not invent reviewers, approvals, deadlines or statutory reporting duties.

## 5 Connect confirmed duties to policy and delivery

Keep legal duties, contractual duties, approved voluntary commitments, verified practice, approved future activity and proposals distinct. A duty does not depend on an executive repeating it. Internal approval cannot waive an external obligation. A legal minimum does not prohibit an organisation from adopting better inclusive practice; label the source of each requirement correctly.

For each supported clause connect authority, intended human outcome, activity, actual/approved process, human owner, evidence, reporting and review. Put unresolved questions and proposed new commitments in the decision log. Do not state that an organisation already complies because it has a policy, a positive survey or a passing scan.

## 6 Review and change control

Have the authorised policy owner coordinate review, with appropriate legal, privacy, technical and operational input. Do not require a lawyer to approve every editorial sentence or postpone an urgent safe response unnecessarily. Pause only affected conclusions/actions when material evidence or authority is absent.

Before adoption verify exact scope, clauses, source dates, reviewer decisions and remaining gaps. Before publication verify the actual approved version and destination. This guide establishes no legal sign-off, automatic monitoring or regulator submission service.
