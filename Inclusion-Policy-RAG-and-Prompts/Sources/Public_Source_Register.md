All listed Double-FW repositories were confirmed public and the named file was retrieved on 17 November 2026. Visibility and access checks do not verify every underlying claim, legal citation or onward dependency. Original source dates and adoption limits still apply.

Every Double-FW entry is **secondary practitioner guidance**. None determines a real organisation's duties. ## Choose sources proportionately

- Discovery: R01 helps identify candidate frameworks from organisational facts. It is a selected inventory, not all law worldwide.
- Conditional: choose R02–R06 and R18 only when the activity, jurisdiction or decision warrants investigation. Their presence in this register does not make their requirements applicable.
- Methods: R07–R17 support participation, ownership, drafting, evaluation and delivery. Recommended methods and local thresholds are not automatically law or approved organisational commitments.
- R06 covers employment and adjustment questions. Accommodation in R18 means rooms/stays, not the entire employment-adjustment process.
- R08's human-accountability scaffold supports delivery; this repository's own RACI remains the policy allocation source. Do not import a second conflicting allocation.

Verify the exact section needed and necessary dependencies before relying on it. If a linked document needs a private or unavailable dependency, use the self-contained method here, a verified public alternative or an explicit unresolved question. Do not reconstruct unseen source content. Private source material is not required by this edition.

## Source records and freshness

For a material claim record source ID/title, exact URL and locator, publisher, version/date, retrieval date, authority type, scope, quotation or qualified paraphrase, and what the claim supports. Record whether it is binding law, a contract, an approved commitment, verified practice or guidance. Preserve differences and contrary evidence.

The machine-readable access/revision inventory is [public-references.json](sources/public-references.json). It records access checks, not adopted baselines or legal review. Recheck public visibility, relevant file availability and current primary authority before a consequential new use. A replaced source needs an impact review, new source record and review of affected clauses.
